Privacy policy

cctv-uploader is used only by its owner, for the owner's own Google account.

  • It uses only the Google Drive drive.file permission. It creates and manages only the files it uploads itself. It cannot see any other files in Drive.
  • The uploaded files are the owner's own camera clips. They stay private in the owner's Drive.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
